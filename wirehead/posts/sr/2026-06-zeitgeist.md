# Zeitgeist: Pristup postaje političko pitanje

## Jun je mesec u kojem AI prelazi iz modela u sistem: država odlučuje ko sme da mu priđe, kompanije gde može da radi, hardver i struja koliko to košta, a testovi proveravaju da li mu se može verovati. Ponuda se širi, ali pristup postaje sve uslovljeniji. Sposobnost više nije dovoljna. Svaki model stiže sa nadležnošću i revizorskim tragom.

Aprilska inteligencija dobila je adresu. U maju je postala podrazumevana. U junu na vrata stiže legitimacija.

Anthropic otvara mesec javnim [modelom Fable 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5 "Anthropic on Fable 5 and Mythos 5") i modelom Mythos 5 iza Project Glasswing-a: porodica modela sada je podeljena na običan i ograničeni vazdušni prostor. Zatim Vašington granicu proizvoda pretvara u granicu po državljanstvu i naređuje [obustavu pristupa modelima Fable i Mythos stranim državljanima](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access?hsLang=en-us "Anthropic on the Fable and Mythos access suspension"). Do 30. juna ograničenje je ukinuto, a Anthropic obećava [globalni pristup modelu Fable i dublja testiranja za vladu](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5 "Anthropic on redeploying Fable 5"). Najnoviji modeli sada stižu sa pasoškom kontrolom.

Ograničeni [pregled GPT-5.6](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/ "OpenAI on the GPT-5.6 limited preview") stiže kroz uži kanal dok Bela kuća traži da kompanija [uspori njegovo puštanje](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/25/the-white-house-is-asking-openai-to-slow-roll-the-release-of-its-new-model-over-safety-concerns/ "TechCrunch on the White House request"). Google tuži [mrežu kineskih sajberkriminalaca optuženu da je koristila Gemini za automatizaciju prevara](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/06/google-sues-chinese-cybercrime-network-that-used-gemini-to-automate-scams/ "Ars Technica on Google's cybercrime lawsuit"), dok Anthropic [optužuje aktere povezane sa Alibabom da su izvlačili sposobnosti Claude-a](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/06/anthropic-claims-alibaba-defied-trump-to-attack-claude-and-steal-capabilities/ "Ars Technica on the Claude extraction allegation") kroz hiljade naloga. Modeli su dokazi, krijumčarena roba i poluga.

Na radnom mestu chat prozor ustupa mesto komandnom centru. Microsoft ugrađuje [identitet, kontekst, pravila i nadzor u agentski sistem](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/06/02/ai-alone-wont-change-your-business-the-system-running-it-will/ "Microsoft on the enterprise agent system"). GitHub dozvoljava da se [Markdown kompajlira u agentske tokove rada](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-11-github-agentic-workflows-is-now-in-public-preview/ "GitHub on Agentic Workflows"), dok ispod svega važi podrazumevani režim samo za čitanje. OpenAI kupuje [Ona](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-ona/ "OpenAI on the Ona acquisition") kako bi uveo dugotrajno izvršavanje u cloudu. Agenti ulaze u [prototip planiranja u Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu](https://deepmind.google/blog/unlocking-uk-house-building-with-ai-accelerated-planning/ "Google DeepMind on AI-assisted planning"), [kalifornijsku javnu nabavku](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/anthropic-and-gov-newsom-forge-deal-allowing-california-government-to-use-claude-at-half-price/ "TechCrunch on California's Claude deal"), [naučne radne površine](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropics-claude-science-bets-on-workflow-not-a-new-model-to-win-over-scientists/ "TechCrunch on Claude Science") i Amazonovu novu [organizaciju inženjera raspoređenih direktno kod klijenata vrednu milijardu dolara](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/amazon-launches-new-1-billion-fde-org-following-openai-and-anthropic/ "TechCrunch on Amazon's FDE organization"). Inteligencije ima. Ono što nedostaje jeste institucionalno pamćenje.

Ispod interfejsa, prostorija se usijava. Google se, prema izveštajima, obavezuje na [$920 miliona mesečno za procesorsku snagu SpaceX-a](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/google-will-pay-spacex-920m-per-month-for-compute/ "TechCrunch on Google's SpaceX compute deal"). AirTrunk planira ulaganje od [$30 milijardi za izgradnju data centara kapaciteta 5 GW u Indiji](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/airtrunk-commits-30b-to-build-5gw-of-ai-data-centers-in-india/ "TechCrunch on AirTrunk's India buildout"). Južnokorejske kompanije odgovaraju na RAMageddon [ulaganjima vrednim više od $550 milijardi](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/south-korean-tech-giants-commit-over-550b-to-ease-ramageddon/ "TechCrunch on South Korea's memory investments"). OpenAI i Broadcom najavljuju [čip za inferencu po meri](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/06/openai-and-broadcom-announce-chip-designed-for-llm-inference-at-scale/ "Ars Technica on the OpenAI-Broadcom chip"), dok Apple pomoću [AFM 3](https://venturebeat.com/technology/on-device-ai-agents-hit-a-hard-memory-limit-apples-new-architecture-routes-around-it "VentureBeat on Apple's AFM 3 architecture") zaobilazi memorijski zid pri pokretanju lokalnog modela od 20 milijardi parametara. Cloud, čip, fleš-memorija, elektroenergetska mreža: stara metafora dobija trafostanice.

Cenovni rat se preliva u stranu. [Gemma 4 12B](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/06/googles-new-gemma-4-open-ai-model-is-sized-for-your-laptop/ "Ars Technica on Gemma 4 12B") i [DiffusionGemma](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/06/googles-latest-diffusiongemma-open-ai-model-comes-with-a-4x-speed-boost/ "Ars Technica on DiffusionGemma") otvaraju put manjoj i bržoj lokalnoj inferenci. DeepSeek je, prema izveštajima, nadomak [runde od $7,4 milijarde](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3355818/deepseek-nears-us7b-haul-first-ever-funding-round-backing-tencent-catl?pgtype=live "SCMP on DeepSeek funding") dok američki kupci pokušavaju da sustignu tu računicu. Anthropic odgovara [jeftinijim Sonnet 5 agentima](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/ "TechCrunch on Claude Sonnet 5"), a Google generisanjem slika za [četiri sekunde i $0,034 na hiljadu slika](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/google-introduces-a-faster-cheaper-image-generator-with-nano-banana-2-lite/ "TechCrunch on Nano Banana 2 Lite"). ChatGPT pada ispod [50 odsto tržišnog udela](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/ "TechCrunch on chatbot market share"), dok DuckDuckGo olakšava pronalaženje [pretrage bez AI-ja](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/duckduckgo-makes-its-no-ai-search-engine-easier-to-access-as-its-traffic-booms/ "TechCrunch on DuckDuckGo's no-AI search"). Inteligencija pojeftinjuje. Pristanak ostaje skup.

Zato se mesec završava problemom dokaza. Patronus gradi [digitalne svetove za stres-testiranje agenata](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/25/patronus-ai-lands-50m-to-build-digital-worlds-that-stress-test-ai-agents/ "TechCrunch on Patronus AI"), a Microsoft Research razvija [testove za agente koji dugo nadziru sisteme](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/sentinelbench-a-benchmark-for-long-running-monitoring-agents/ "Microsoft Research on SentinelBench"). Ford, prema izveštajima, [ponovo zapošljava iskusne inženjere nakon što je AI podbacio u kontroli kvaliteta](https://thenextweb.com/news/ford-rehired-350-engineers-ai-quality-jd-power "The Next Web on Ford's engineering rehires"). Agent može da preduzima radnje. Institucija i dalje mora da zna šta je dotakao, koliko je to koštalo i ko može da vrati stvari unazad.

Signal juna nije autonomija. To je uslovna autonomija: inteligencija sa kapijom, brojilom, nadzornikom i nadležnošću. Mašina i dalje prolazi kroz zid. Zid uči da joj ispostavlja račun.
