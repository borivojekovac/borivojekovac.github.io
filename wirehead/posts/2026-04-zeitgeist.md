# Zeitgeist: Intelligence With An Address

## April gives intelligence a street address: cloud zones, classified networks, desktop agents, photo libraries, power plants. The model race keeps flashing, but the deeper signal is emplacement. AI is no longer just capability. It is permission, supply, memory, and jurisdiction.

The month opens with the frontier splintering into specialties. Google drops [Gemma 4 under Apache 2.0](https://huggingface.co/blog/gemma4 "Hugging Face on Gemma 4"), multimodal and local-minded, threaded through transformers, llama.cpp, MLX, WebGPU, Rust. Intelligence as portable machinery. Anthropic answers with [Claude Opus 4.7](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7 "Anthropic on Claude Opus 4.7"), tuned for long work, agentic coding, vision, finance, the slow burn of tasks that do not fit in a demo. A week later OpenAI ships [GPT-5.5](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/ "OpenAI on GPT-5.5"), folding model, tools, ChatGPT, and Codex closer into one nervous system. [Kimi K2.6 runs agents for days](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/kimi-k2-6-runs-agents-for-days-and-exposes-the-limits-of-enterprise-orchestration "VentureBeat on Kimi K2.6"), Google's TurboQuant promises [up to 6x lower memory use](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/04/google-turboquant-promises-6x-lower-llm-memory-usage-with-no-quality-loss/ "Ars Technica on TurboQuant"). The leaderboard dissolves into a parts catalog: open, closed, cheap, durable, local, scientific, dangerous.

The dangerous part gets a name. Anthropic keeps [Claude Mythos Preview inside Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/project/glasswing "Anthropic on Project Glasswing"), handing it to selected security partners instead of the public internet. Axios reports a model that can [find and exploit security flaws autonomously](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/07/anthropic-mythos-preview-cybersecurity-risks "Axios on Claude Mythos risks"). This is safety as a patch window: seal the cracks in private before the street learns how to exploit them. At the same time, Google expands [Pentagon access to its AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/28/google-expands-pentagons-access-to-its-ai-after-anthropics-refusal/ "TechCrunch on Google's Pentagon AI access") after Anthropic refuses broader terms. The state is not outside the system now. It is a buyer, pressure gradient, exception handler.

Agents leave the chat window and start touching the furniture. OpenAI updates Codex so it can [operate desktop apps, generate images, preview webpages, and use more than 90 plugins](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-drastically-updates-codex-desktop-app-to-use-all-other-apps-on-your-computer-generate-images-preview-webpages "VentureBeat on Codex desktop update"). Then come [Workspace Agents](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/openai-unveils-workspace-agents-a-successor-to-custom-gpts-for-enterprises-that-can-plug-directly-into-slack-salesforce-and-more "VentureBeat on OpenAI Workspace Agents"), wired into Slack, Salesforce, Google Drive, Microsoft apps, Notion, Atlassian. GitHub makes [Copilot CLI generally available](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/04/github-copilot-cli-ga/ "InfoQ on GitHub Copilot CLI"), AWS ships a [DevOps Agent for incident investigation](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/04/aws-devops-agent-ga/ "InfoQ on AWS DevOps Agent"). The new interface is not conversational. It is operational.

But operation needs discipline, and April exposes the failure surface. VentureBeat reports [72% of organizations claiming multiple primary AI platforms](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/the-ai-governance-mirage-why-72-of-enterprises-dont-have-the-control-and-security-they-think-they-do "VentureBeat on enterprise AI governance gaps"), a polite phrase for sprawl with credentials. Another survey says [43% of AI-generated code changes need production debugging](https://venturebeat.com/technology/43-of-ai-generated-code-changes-need-debugging-in-production-survey-finds "VentureBeat on AI-generated code debugging"). Autonomy has a heat signature. It burns tokens, permissions, attention.

Under it all, the physical machine grows teeth. OpenAI says Stargate has [already passed its 10GW US infrastructure target](https://openai.com/index/building-the-compute-infrastructure-for-the-intelligence-age/ "OpenAI on Stargate infrastructure"), adding more than 3GW in 90 days. Google debuts [new Tensor chips split between training and inference](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/22/google-unifies-gemini-enterprise-debuts-chips "Axios on Google's new TPU generation"). Google reportedly commits [up to $40 billion for Anthropic](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/google-to-invest-up-to-40b-in-anthropic-in-cash-and-compute/ "TechCrunch on Google's Anthropic investment"), Meta signs [a multi-year Amazon chip deal](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/24/meta-deal-amazon-chips "Axios on Meta and Amazon chips"), natural gas plant costs [jump 66% on data center demand](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/27/data-center-demand-drives-66-surge-in-natural-gas-power-plant-costs/ "TechCrunch on data center power plant costs"). The cloud stops being a metaphor. It has substations.

Consumer AI becomes less spectacular and more intimate. Adobe's [Firefly AI Assistant](https://venturebeat.com/technology/adobes-new-firefly-ai-assistant-wants-to-run-photoshop-premiere-illustrator-and-more-from-one-prompt "VentureBeat on Adobe Firefly AI Assistant") wants to drive Creative Cloud from a prompt. Anthropic launches [Claude Design](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-design-anthropic-labs "Anthropic on Claude Design"). Google lets Gemini [dig through Photos for personalized image generation](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/04/gemini-can-now-create-personalized-ai-images-by-digging-around-in-google-photos/ "Ars Technica on Gemini and Google Photos"). ComfyUI hits a [$500 million valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/comfyui-hits-500m-valuation-as-creators-seek-more-control-over-ai-generated-media/ "TechCrunch on ComfyUI valuation") because creators still want handles, not just wishes.

April's shape is emplacement. Intelligence gets installed: in terminals, dashboards, classified networks, creative suites, cars, personal archives, power markets. The magic word is not "model" anymore. It is "access."
